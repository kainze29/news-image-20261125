One picture of one day. The front page in `news.jpg` covers Wednesday 25 November 2026 in 51 stories, every one of them dated that day by its publisher's own timestamp.

## What is on the page

- Six photo stories up top: tankers burning in the Strait of Hormuz, Pakistan's first astronaut reaching Tiangong, Ukraine sinking a Russian warship off Libya, Huawei's conviction in a US trade-secrets case, Nvidia's 7% slide as the AI sell-off spreads, and every hostage freed in the Cartagena supermarket siege.
- A numbered brief of 45 more stories, from eurozone inflation to a record 204 off 53 balls by a 15-year-old cricketer.
- A world map dotted with where it all happened, plus a date check and the sources.

## How to run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

The script checks the image fits the limits (JPEG, 1024x1024, under 1 MiB) and copies it to `out/entry.png`. It writes the same bytes on every run.
